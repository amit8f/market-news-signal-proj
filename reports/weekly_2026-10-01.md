## POST-HOC / NOT PRE-REGISTERED (QA notes added by hand after the run)

- **Independent recomputation:** per-signal raw and market-adjusted returns were recomputed with a separate code
  path (pandas window slicing instead of index search). Pooled means match the tables above to the reported
  precision overall and for every class (n=1200). Decision-time matching: 0 duplicate keys, 1200/1200 matched.
- **Extremes:** market-adjusted 2h returns range -3.9% .. +3.8% (1st..99th percentile -1.6% .. +2.2%); the
  largest moves (INTC, AMD, GS, META) are ordinary intraday moves, not split or bad-bar artifacts.
- **The random-ticker control is centred below zero for a composition reason.** An equal-weight basket of all
  40 universe tickers trailed SPY by -0.035% per window over 150 sampled signal windows, matching the control
  median (-0.042%). Signals cluster in mega-caps that track SPY closely (NVDA 14%, META 12%, AAPL 9% of
  signals), so the "all signals" 97.5th percentile mostly reflects *which tickers get news*, not information
  from the model. The control as registered does not match on ticker size; read its percentiles with that
  in mind.
- **The control distribution varies only the random draw, not the days.** It does not include sampling
  uncertainty in the real mean, so a high percentile is weaker evidence than it looks; the real mean's own
  day-bootstrap CI (section (a)) is the better guide, and every such CI here includes zero.
- Decision time trails headline publish time by a median 29.5 min (95th percentile 57.4 min), which is why
  windows start at decision time rather than publish time.
